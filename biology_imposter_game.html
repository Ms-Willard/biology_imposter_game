🧬 Biology Imposter

Cell Biology Edition • 4–5 players • One hidden imposter

Set Up Your Group

Choose 4 or 5 players. Pass one device around so each player can secretly reveal their role.

Vocabulary in This Game

How to Play

  • Everyone except the imposter receives the same biology vocabulary word.
  • Give one short clue describing the word without saying the word itself.
  • After everyone gives a clue, discuss and vote for the imposter.
  • The vocabulary set is fixed to 20 cell-biology terms: Cell, Prokaryote, Eukaryote, Nucleus, Organelle, Plasma membrane, Cell wall, Chloroplast, Cilia, Cytoplasm, Cytoskeleton, Endoplasmic reticulum (smooth/rough), Vesicles, Flagella, Golgi apparatus, Lysosome, Mitochondria, Ribosome, Vacuole, and Chlorophyll.

Secret Role

Memorize your card. Then hide it before passing the device.

All roles have been revealed.

Clue Round

Give one-word clue. Do not say the vocabulary word.

Vote

Discuss the clues, then select the player you think is the imposter.
